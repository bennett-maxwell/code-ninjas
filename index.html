Franchise Opportunity Profile

Code Ninjas

Code Ninjas is a child-focused education franchise teaching kids coding, programming, math, and STEM skills. Every fact on this page is sourced from the Code Ninjas, LLC 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document (issued April 3, 2026), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$45,000

Learning Center, uniform, non-refundable. Studio Center $35,000. 10% veteran discount. Last-year fees collected: $20,000-$40,000. (FDD Item 5, p.6)

Estimated Initial Investment

$174,250 to $265,750

$45,750 of that payable to the franchisor or affiliate. Studio Center: $73,500-$109,250. (FDD Item 7, pp.15-18)

Royalty

8.25% of Net Sales

Learning Center. Studio Center: 12.25% of Net Sales. Collected through the franchisor's payment processor at the time of sale. (FDD Item 6, p.7)

Marketing Fund

2.0% of Net Sales

To the Brand Marketing Development Fund. Mandatory local spend: $2,000-$2,500/mo for 24 months, then 4.0% of Net Sales. Plus $350/mo in-house agency + $350/mo tech fee. (FDD Item 6, p.7; Item 11, p.29)

Territory

Protected — not exclusive

Typical Protected Area: 1.5 to 2 mile radius; no other Learning Center inside it while compliant. No sales quota. Franchisor retains all other rights. (FDD Item 12, pp.34-35)

Term

10 years, 3x 10-year renewals

Renewal fee $3,500; general release required; post-term non-compete 2 years. Mediation then litigation; Georgia law. (FDD Item 17, pp.41-47)

Facility

1,100 to 1,500 sq ft

Learning Center; build-out ~$85-$150/sq ft. Studio format: under 1,000 sq ft or hosted in schools/community spaces with a 12-month commitment. (FDD Item 1, p.3; Item 7, note 4, p.19)

Training

Initial Management Training

$750 per attendee; Atlanta metro; ~4 days. Center Director must complete training; Designated Principal must hold 10%+ equity. (FDD Item 11; Item 5, p.6)

Time to Open

By Opening Deadline

Miss the agreed opening date and pay $500/month minimum royalty until open; termination rights retained. (FDD Item 5, p.6)

Owner Model

Hands-on required

Must participate in day-to-day management; full-time Center Director required if the Designated Principal is not full-time on site. (FDD Item 15, p.40)

HQ

Peachtree Corners, Georgia

Code Ninjas, LLC — Texas LLC — 3500 Parkway Lane, Suite 400, Peachtree Corners, GA 30092. 855.446.4652. (FDD Item 1, p.1)

System Size

245 outlets

238 franchised + 7 company-owned as of 12/31/2025, across 35 states. Net change: -22 (2023), -23 (2024), -1 (2025). (FDD Item 20, p.52)

Risk Profile & Disclosure

36 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Moderate-low. A mature, child-education system with Item 19 reporting and a clean litigation record, tempered by a contracting outlet base (284 to 238 franchised outlets since 2023), high closure activity (41 closures in 2024), ongoing fees of 10.25% of net sales plus substantial fixed monthly fees, and private-equity ownership with no franchisor financing offered.

Risk inputs (documented):

  • Outlet contraction: franchised outlets fell 284 → 238 (−16.2%) from 2023 to 2025 — pushes score up
  • Attrition: 2024 saw 8 terminations + 20 non-renewals (28 of 262 start-year outlets); 2025 saw 13 closures — pushes score up
  • Ongoing fee burden: 8.25% royalty + 2.0% marketing = 10.25% of net sales, plus $2,000–$2,500/mo local spend (first 24 months, then 4% of net sales), $350/mo tech fee, $350/mo agency fee, $8,000 grand-opening minimum — pushes score up
  • Capital intensity: investment ceiling $265,750 with $45,750 payable to the franchisor or affiliate — pushes score up
  • PE control: Eagle Merchant Partners is the ultimate manager; no franchisor financing — pushes score up
  • Item 19 disclosed: 224 outlets, 2025 average annual gross sales $235,927 (sales-only, no profit) — pushes score down
  • Audited financial statements attached (Exhibit G, FY2023–2025) — pushes score down
  • Clean legal record: no litigation and no bankruptcy disclosed (Items 3–4) — pushes score down

Method: base 20 + 6 outlet contraction + 6 high attrition + 5 ongoing fee burden + 4 capital intensity + 2 PE control − 3 Item 19 disclosed − 2 audited financials − 2 clean litigation/bankruptcy record = 36. Published so an independent verifier can recompute it exactly.
